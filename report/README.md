# Reports

The Phase 6 write-ups, produced once the pipeline was built and proven. Together they
present the build as a set of controls: the technical record, the mapping to
recognised frameworks, and the business case.

- [`pipeline-security-report.md`](pipeline-security-report.md): each gate in order,
  the control it enforces, the planted weakness it was proven against, the failing and
  passing runs with evidence, and a gate-by-gate scorecard.
- [`controls-mapping.md`](controls-mapping.md): each gate mapped to NIST SSDF, OWASP
  SAMM and DSOMM, and the change-management, segregation-of-duties and audit-trail IT
  general controls, with the lab's limitations stated.
- [`business-report.md`](business-report.md): a business and risk reader's view,
  framed against UK operational resilience (PRA and FCA) and SOX-style IT general
  controls, with a layers-of-defence diagram.

The per-phase evidence these reports cite is under [`evidence/`](../evidence/), and
the phase guides are in [`docs/`](../docs/README.md). The phase guide for this report
is [docs/06-report.md](../docs/06-report.md).
