-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The image that passed the Phase 4 gates is signed, the signature verifies against
the pipeline's own workflow identity (not a stored key), and its provenance ties the
image digest to the exact source commit and workflow run. Verification succeeds only
for this identity and issuer, so an unsigned image, or one signed elsewhere, fails
the same check.
